# Comprehensive opportunity audit — October 12, 2026

Examined all **102 existing records** (98 published, four archived), including the records imported since the previous weekly review. ## Method and limits

Refreshed every stored official URL, including archived records, then obtained linked eligibility, FAQ, benefits, application and PDF material and rendered browser fallbacks. 199 unique source URLs attempted through direct retrieval; additional web sources and browser pages are cited per record. Working URLs alone did not count as verification. Source text was treated as data. Same-day completed reviews from the morning report were retained and source retrieval refreshed; the deeper pass resolved additional program terms and corrected regressions introduced by the earlier artifact import.

Inspected clean main and open PRs (none). Preserved all original IDs and archival states. No records deleted/reactivated, email sent, staff contacted, secrets inspected or services purchased. Only completed substantive reviews advance verified dates; partial/blocked records retain the baseline (new partial records have no complete verified date). Records with no new dated call remain explicitly unconfirmed. Approximate city map points represent potential sites, not guaranteed assignments or geocoded facilities.

English/Spanish summaries and structured fields were checked for consistent audience, status and scope. Existing prep kits retained where still consistent. Official sources govern discrepancies. Dates and pay are not inferred from annual recurrence, vague ranges, old postings or blocked pages.

DoW Opportunity Finder: weekly digest

Week of October 5, 2026. Full list: https://xuwenwu.github.io/dow-opportunity-finder/

New this week

  • Army Educational Outreach Program internships and fellowships
    Paid research internships for undergraduates and fellowships for graduate students and postdocs at Army labs and centers.
  • The Aerospace Corporation internships (deadline February 26, 2027)
    Summer internships supporting national security space programs, in 14 or more disciplines from aerospace engineering to cybersecurity.
  • Applied Research Laboratories student jobs and research assistantships
    Paid part time and summer research jobs for undergraduates and graduate research assistantships at the Navy affiliated lab on the UT Austin campus.
  • Defense Civilian Training Corps at the University of Arizona
    Two year scholarship for juniors and seniors with DoW focused courses and summer internships; the University of Arizona is one of four pilot schools.
  • Construction Engineering Research Laboratory internships
    Research internships at the Army Corps of Engineers laboratory for military facilities, energy and construction engineering.
  • General Atomics summer internships
    Ten week summer internships from June to August in engineering, science and business, including aeronautical and electromagnetic systems.
  • Los Alamos National Laboratory student programs
    Undergraduate and graduate student appointments at the national security lab in northern New Mexico.
  • Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory computing summer internships (deadline January 15, 2027)
    Paid summer internships in computing at a national security laboratory near UC Davis, UC Merced and UC Santa Cruz; the lab also posts science and engineering internships.
  • NAWCAD STEM Student Employment Program
    Paid year round student employment at Navy aircraft engineering sites.
  • NMSU PSL — Classified Ready Employee Workforce (CREW)
    NMSU national-security career preparation combining practical experience, seminars and possible Secret-clearance sponsorship. NMSU reported DoD Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office support in 2025.
  • Nevada National Security Site student program
    Twelve week paid summer internships, in person or hybrid; about 60 percent of 2025 interns kept working part time during the school year.
  • Northrop Grumman summer internships
    Summer internships across Northrop Grumman sites in engineering, cyber, business and more.
  • Naval Postgraduate School internships (NREIP host) (deadline November 30, 2026)
    Summer research with NPS faculty through NREIP; NPS also runs community college internships with Hartnell College and Monterey Peninsula College.
  • RTX Raytheon internships in Tucson
    Summer engineering internships at Raytheon's missile systems headquarters near the University of Arizona.
  • Sandia National Laboratories internships
    Paid internships in engineering, science and computing at the national security lab in Albuquerque and Livermore.
  • Southwest Research Institute summer internships
    Summer internships on real projects at one of the largest independent research labs, much of it defense and space work.
  • UCF Intelligence Community Center for Academic Excellence awards
    Study abroad scholarships, internship support awards, student research awards and travel stipends for UCF students preparing for national security careers.
  • Army Institute of Surgical Research summer research
    Undergraduate STEM research with combat casualty care research teams, including journal clubs, seminars and a poster presentation.
  • Test Resource Management Center (TRMC) internships at DoW test ranges (deadline November 30, 2026)
    Paid 10 week summer internships at DoW test ranges across the country, with housing and travel covered for eligible interns. Past White Sands interns included UTEP and NMSU students.
  • ONR Sabbatical Leave Program
    Residential Navy laboratory research for one semester to one year. Student participation: no accompanying-student award is promised; discuss separate student internships and project coordination with the laboratory before planning a team visit.
  • DEVCOM Army Research Laboratory Faculty Fellowship (RAP)
    Faculty research appointments with Army Research Laboratory scientists; applications are accepted throughout the year. Student participation: ARL has a separate Summer Student Experience, but the faculty award does not guarantee student places or placement together.
  • HPCMP Faculty Immersion Experience (FIX)
    Ten-week summer faculty research at DoW host organizations using high-performance computing. Students apply separately through the companion HPC Internship Program (HIP); a shared mentor or location must be confirmed. Next application cycle is not verified.
  • Naval Engineering Education Consortium (NEEC) faculty-student research
    University grants for faculty-led naval engineering research with undergraduate or graduate students and Navy collaborators. This is a team research grant, not a visiting fellowship; on-site student work requires host arrangements. FY2026 call remains forecasted.
  • VICEROY Scholars — MAVEN and ENVOY Internships
    Cybersecurity and electromagnetic spectrum training through VICEROY academic partners, with paid MAVEN and ENVOY summer internships at defense host sites. Includes research, mentoring and leadership development.
  • SCALE — Microelectronics Research and Career Pathways
    Scalable Asymmetric Lifecycle Engagement (SCALE) connects students with semiconductor and microelectronics research, mentoring, internships and targeted scholarships. Topics include advanced packaging, radiation hardening, chip design and trusted AI.
  • UC President’s Postdoctoral Fellowship — National Laboratories (deadline November 1, 2026)
    UC-sponsored STEM postdoctoral research at Lawrence Berkeley, Lawrence Livermore or Los Alamos, with mentoring, professional development and networking. Apply through the central PPFP portal and identify a host-laboratory technical adviser.
  • HPCMP High Performance Computing Internship Program (HIP)
    Ten-week mentored research in high-performance computing at defense research organizations. Supports STEM students and recent graduates working on computational science and engineering. The 2026 call is closed; next-cycle dates are not announced.
  • AFRL Repperger Research Intern Program
    Mentored summer research in human performance, biomedical engineering, sensors, optics and related STEM fields at AFRL. The completed 2026 cycle included Wright-Patterson AFB and Fort Sam Houston; next-cycle projects and dates are not announced.
  • Naval Health Research Center, San Diego (NREIP host) (deadline November 30, 2026)
    Paid NREIP research in warfighter health, human performance, injury prevention, biomedical engineering and medical data science at Naval Base Point Loma. Select NHRC through the NREIP application.
  • NSWC Port Hueneme Division (NREIP host) (deadline November 30, 2026)
    Paid NREIP experience in ship combat systems, systems engineering, modeling and simulation, and test and evaluation. Select NSWC Port Hueneme through the NREIP application.
  • Lockheed Martin defense internships
    Student internships at a defense contractor. Choose a defense or national-security assignment and check its specific requirements.
  • BAE Systems U.S. LEAP internships and co-ops
    U.S. LEAP placements in engineering, cyber, manufacturing and business at a defense contractor. Internships and co-ops are separate from graduate leadership jobs.
  • L3Harris defense internships
    Student internships in defense technology, with mentoring and project experience across engineering and business functions. Apply to an individual U.S. vacancy.
  • General Dynamics Mission Systems internships
    Paid summer experience on defense and security technologies at General Dynamics Mission Systems.
  • HII defense internships — shipbuilding and Mission Technologies
    Paid placements at Ingalls Shipbuilding, Newport News Shipbuilding and Mission Technologies, supporting naval and other defense work.
  • Booz Allen Summer Games — defense and national-security technology
    Ten-week team internship developing technology prototypes for mission challenges, with mentors and a final presentation.
  • CACI national-security internships and co-ops
    Year-round student placements supporting national-security technology, software, engineering and related missions.
  • Peraton national-security internships and co-ops
    Student work on national-security customer projects; summer internships and selected semester co-ops with mentoring.
  • SNC aerospace and national-security internships
    Engineering, cyber, manufacturing and business projects supporting aerospace, defense and national-security missions.
  • Textron Systems defense internships
    Student placements at the defense-focused Textron Systems business. Search the student portal for current engineering and other vacancies.
  • AeroVironment defense-technology internships
    Paid projects in autonomy, robotics, software, hardware, manufacturing, space and cyber at a defense-technology employer.
  • Shield AI software engineering internship — summer 2027
    On-site software work on autonomous defense systems, including applications, embedded software, machine learning and simulation.
  • Anduril engineering co-ops — winter 2027
    Winter 2027 co-op vacancies in mechanical, electrical, manufacturing, systems, software and other engineering functions at a defense-technology company.
  • Boeing / Spirit Defense IT and cybersecurity internship — summer 2027
    Paid internship supporting secure applications, cyber, infrastructure and data systems used by defense and space programs.
  • MIT Lincoln Laboratory Summer Research Program
    Paid research on advanced technologies relevant to national security. This summer program accepts students from beyond MIT.
  • Johns Hopkins APL national-defense research internships
    Defense opportunities include 2027 shipboard systems, military cybersecurity and systems engineering internships. Select a defense assignment from the broader APL board.
  • MITRE National Security Intern Program
    Mission-focused student pathway supporting defense and intelligence sponsors, with mentoring, technical learning and potential clearance sponsorship.
  • Draper defense-research internships and co-ops
    Undergraduate and graduate research placements. Choose a defense or national-security project; these are separate from the graduate Scholars award.
  • Draper Scholars graduate fellowship — awaiting next cycle
    Graduate thesis research addressing national-security challenges with university and Draper co-advisers. The 2026 admissions cycle is closed.
  • SWE RTX Scholarship — awaiting next cycle
    Engineering/computing scholarship sponsored by RTX, with preference for HBCU/HSI enrollment. The 2026–27 SWE application cycle is closed.
  • SNC STEM Scholarship — awaiting next cycle
    Scholarship and SNC mentor for nonmedical STEM students, funded through the Ozmen Foundation. Current applications are closed.
  • Shield AI Future Innovators Scholarship — awaiting next cycle
    For graduating high-school seniors interested in AI, autonomy, aviation, robotics and related STEM. Applications closed April 15, 2026.

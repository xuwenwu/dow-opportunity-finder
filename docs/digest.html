DoW Opportunity Finder: weekly digest

Week of October 12, 2026. Full list: https://xuwenwu.github.io/dow-opportunity-finder/

New this week

  • Air Force ASSURE undergraduate research (at NSF REU sites)
    Paid summer research for undergraduates in Defense-relevant fields at NSF REU sites that the Air Force co-funds. Students apply directly to each REU site.
  • Air Force Premier College Intern Program (PCIP)
    Paid summer internship at Air Force sites that can lead to full-time civilian jobs through PALACE Acquire or COPPER CAP after graduation. Fields include STEM, cyber, acquisition and intelligence.
  • AFRL Minority Leaders Research Collaboration Program (ML-RCP)
    AFRL research funding for faculty and students at HBCUs and Minority Serving Institutions, including HSIs, typically $50,000 to $125,000 per year per project. Membership is free and students may get paid summer research at AFRL.
  • AFRL Scholars at Space Systems Command (SSC/CGN), San Diego (deadline January 11, 2027)
    Paid summer research internship with U.S. Space Force Space Systems Command at Naval Base Point Loma in San Diego. The site focuses on MUOS, a Defense Department satellite communications system that uses UHF and WCDMA technology.
  • Anduril Industries 2027 internships
    Paid 12 week summer 2027 internships in software, mechanical, electrical and other engineering at the defense technology company Anduril, with offices in Costa Mesa and Irvine and in Colorado.
  • AscendR Research faculty grants
    Competitive one year grants of up to $200,000 for early and mid-career faculty at HSRU member schools to do defense-aligned research with at least one graduate student. Winners also join the AscendR Leadership program.
  • DoW Multidisciplinary University Research Initiative (MURI, FY2027) (deadline April 9, 2027)
    Large multi-year grants for university teams doing basic research across several disciplines on DoW priority topics. Awards are up to $1.5 million per year for up to five years.
  • Intelligence Community Postdoctoral Research Fellowship Program
    Postdoctoral fellowship for new PhDs to do research at a U.S. university or government lab with an Intelligence Community advisor. Pays a yearly stipend plus travel and lab funds.
  • Los Alamos Dynamics Summer School (deadline January 2, 2027)
    Paid dynamics research summer school at Los Alamos, May 31-August 6, 2027. Fellowships are $12,000-$18,000. Early review closes November 14, 2026; general applications close January 2, 2027.
  • Los Alamos Quantum Computing Summer School Fellowship
    Ten week paid summer fellowship at Los Alamos with two weeks of quantum computing lectures and eight weeks of mentored research that includes hands on programming of a quantum computer. About 20 fellows are chosen.
  • Los Alamos Remote Sensing Summer School (deadline January 10, 2027)
    Paid remote-sensing research for up to ten graduate students, June 7-August 13, 2027. Apply October 12, 2026-January 10, 2027; selection is rolling. Official citizenship wording conflicts and needs confirmation.
  • NSA Summer Internships (Summer 2027)
    Paid 12 week summer internships at NSA in STEM, language, intelligence analysis and mission support fields. Interns may get subsidized housing and travel reimbursement.
  • NSWC Port Hueneme STEM Student Employment Program (SSEP)
    Paid Navy civilian student job for STEM majors at NSWC Port Hueneme and its sites in San Diego and White Sands. Students work at least 8 weeks each summer and can keep working until they graduate.
  • Army SMDC Technical Center STEM Internship (Summer 2027) (deadline October 31, 2026)
    Paid summer research internship in Army space and missile defense labs at Redstone Arsenal. Open to undergraduate, graduate and postdoctoral students nationwide.
  • USC Institute for Creative Technologies Summer Research Program
    Paid 12 week summer research internship at USC's Army UARC, on projects such as simulation and training technology for the Army. Positions are posted each year on the ICT internship page.

Closing in the next 30 days

  • NDSEG Fellowship (deadline October 30, 2026)
    National Defense Science and Engineering Graduate (NDSEG) Fellowship: three years of support for doctoral study in science and engineering aligned with defense research.
  • ONR Young Investigator Program (FY2027) (deadline October 30, 2026)
    Early career award for faculty doing Navy relevant research.
  • Army SMDC Technical Center STEM Internship (Summer 2027) (deadline October 31, 2026)
    Paid summer research internship in Army space and missile defense labs at Redstone Arsenal. Open to undergraduate, graduate and postdoctoral students nationwide.
  • UC President’s Postdoctoral Fellowship, National Laboratories (deadline November 1, 2026)
    UC-sponsored STEM postdoctoral research at Lawrence Berkeley, Lawrence Livermore or Los Alamos, with mentoring, professional development and networking. Apply through the central PPFP portal and identify a host-laboratory technical adviser.
  • NRC Research Associateship Programs (deadline November 2, 2026)
    Postdoctoral and senior research awards at federal laboratories; search the listings for defense laboratory hosts.
  • Defense University Research Instrumentation Program (DURIP, FY2027) (deadline November 6, 2026)
    Equipment and instrumentation for university research of interest to the DoW.

Each listing summarizes the official program page. The official page is the final word on eligibility and deadlines.

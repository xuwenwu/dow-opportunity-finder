Closing in the next 30 days

  • NAWC Training Systems Division (NREIP host) (deadline November 30, 2026)
    Navy center for training and simulation systems that hosts NREIP interns.
  • Naval Health Research Center, San Diego (NREIP host) (deadline November 30, 2026)
    Paid NREIP summer research at the Naval Health Research Center, Point Loma, San Diego, in operational readiness, military population health and operational infectious diseases, drawing on physiology, biomedical engineering, epidemiology, psychology and software engineering. Select NHRC through the NREIP application.
  • NIWC Pacific student internships (deadline November 30, 2026)
    San Diego Navy lab that hosts NREIP interns and hires STEM students through its Student Educational Employment Program and SDSU Research Foundation postings.
  • Naval Postgraduate School internships (NREIP host) (deadline November 30, 2026)
    Paid summer research with NPS faculty through NREIP; the 2027 project catalog is available. NPS also has separate community-college and unpaid volunteer pathways with different rules.
  • Naval Research Enterprise Internship Program (NREIP) (deadline November 30, 2026)
    Ten-week paid summer research at Navy laboratories nationwide. Citizenship rules depend on the host; NRL Washington, DC now excludes permanent residents.
  • NSWC Corona Division (NREIP host) (deadline November 30, 2026)
    Navy warfare center for systems assessment, measurement science and range engineering; takes NREIP interns in computer science, electrical engineering, physics and systems engineering.
  • NSWC Port Hueneme Division (NREIP host) (deadline November 30, 2026)
    Paid NREIP summer research at Naval Surface Warfare Center Port Hueneme Division, a Navy warfare center supporting surface fleet combat systems, test and evaluation, and cybersecurity. Select NSWC Port Hueneme through the NREIP application.
  • Test Resource Management Center (TRMC) internships at DoW test ranges (deadline November 30, 2026)
    Paid 10 week summer internships at DoW test ranges across the country, with housing and travel covered for eligible interns. Past White Sands interns included UTEP and NMSU students.
  • SMART Scholarship for Service (deadline December 4, 2026)
    Full scholarship plus paid summer internships, followed by guaranteed civilian employment at a DoW facility.

Each listing summarizes the official program page. The official page is the final word on eligibility and deadlines.

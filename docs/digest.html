DoW Opportunity Finder: weekly digest

Week of September 29, 2026. Full list: https://xuwenwu.github.io/dow-opportunity-finder/

New this week

  • Army Educational Outreach Program internships and fellowships
    Paid research internships for undergraduates and fellowships for graduate students and postdocs at Army labs and centers.
  • The Aerospace Corporation internships (deadline February 26, 2027)
    Summer internships supporting national security space programs, in 14 or more disciplines from aerospace engineering to cybersecurity.
  • Applied Research Laboratories student jobs and research assistantships
    Paid part time and summer research jobs for undergraduates and graduate research assistantships at the Navy affiliated lab on the UT Austin campus.
  • Defense Civilian Training Corps at the University of Arizona
    Two year scholarship for juniors and seniors with DoW focused courses and summer internships; the University of Arizona is one of four pilot schools.
  • Construction Engineering Research Laboratory internships
    Research internships at the Army Corps of Engineers laboratory for military facilities, energy and construction engineering.
  • General Atomics summer internships
    Ten week summer internships from June to August in engineering, science and business, including aeronautical and electromagnetic systems.
  • Los Alamos National Laboratory student programs
    Undergraduate and graduate student appointments at the national security lab in northern New Mexico.
  • Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory computing summer internships (deadline January 15, 2027)
    Paid summer internships in computing at a national security laboratory near UC Davis, UC Merced and UC Santa Cruz; the lab also posts science and engineering internships.
  • NAWCAD STEM Student Employment Program
    Paid year round student employment at Navy aircraft engineering sites.
  • NMSU Classified Ready Employee Workforce (CREW)
    Career pathway into national security work: hands on experience, a national security affairs seminar and security clearance initiation; more than 157 students prepared so far.
  • Nevada National Security Site student program
    Twelve week paid summer internships, in person or hybrid; about 60 percent of 2025 interns kept working part time during the school year.
  • Northrop Grumman summer internships
    Summer internships across Northrop Grumman sites in engineering, cyber, business and more.
  • Naval Postgraduate School internships (NREIP host) (deadline November 30, 2026)
    Summer research with NPS faculty through NREIP; NPS also runs community college internships with Hartnell College and Monterey Peninsula College.
  • RTX Raytheon internships in Tucson
    Summer engineering internships at Raytheon's missile systems headquarters near the University of Arizona.
  • Sandia National Laboratories internships
    Paid internships in engineering, science and computing at the national security lab in Albuquerque and Livermore.
  • Southwest Research Institute summer internships
    Summer internships on real projects at one of the largest independent research labs, much of it defense and space work.
  • UCF Intelligence Community Center for Academic Excellence awards
    Study abroad scholarships, internship support awards, student research awards and travel stipends for UCF students preparing for national security careers.
  • Army Institute of Surgical Research summer research
    Undergraduate STEM research with combat casualty care research teams, including journal clubs, seminars and a poster presentation.
  • Test Resource Management Center (TRMC) internships at DoW test ranges (deadline November 30, 2026)
    Paid 10 week summer internships at DoW test ranges across the country, with housing and travel covered for eligible interns. Past White Sands interns included UTEP and NMSU students.

Closing in the next 30 days

No deadlines in the next 30 days.
